About

Liam Rodgers

I'm Liam Rodgers, a high school junior in Charleston, South Carolina. I'm interested in how people make financial decisions, from a family's budget to a company's acquisition.

I've been exploring that through shadowing a Certified Financial Planner, building a mock plan in RightCapital, writing research tools in Python, and talking with people across finance.

I called this site Compounding because I want to see what consistent study adds up to. Writing gives me something to revisit: what I thought, what I tested, and where I need to look again.

Start with my acquisition study, the review of my paper-trading account, or my first mock financial plan.

You can find me on LinkedIn or through the contact page.
